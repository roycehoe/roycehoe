I built [Open Hansard](https://openhansard.sg), a free, unofficial archive of Singapore's parliamentary record since 1955. Pick an MP and read what they've said. Some speeches name only a role, so [linking them to a member means checking who held it on that sitting date](https://github.com/roycehoe/hansard-scraper#how-it-was-built).

I co-founded [Parleh-mate](https://github.com/parleh-mate) in 2023 and wrote its first scraper.

I co-led the four-person team behind [Heartbeat](https://github.com/roycehoe/heartbeat), a daily check-in for seniors who live alone. If a senior misses a day, their caregiver gets a WhatsApp message. It began at Open Government Products' Build for Good 2024, and [The Straits Times wrote about it](https://www.straitstimes.com/singapore/community/sporeans-come-together-to-build-apps-for-the-good-of-caregivers-and-isolated-seniors).

I get [AI agents to arrange music for string quartet](https://github.com/roycehoe/music-scores). They can't hear it, so I do the listening.

Mostly Python, Postgres and auth.
